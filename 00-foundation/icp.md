# Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) — Nergi

## One-person sketch

**Maxo, 55, a small-scale farmer in a village in western Georgia.**

Maxo grows vegetables and other crops on his own land and depends on the harvest for income. He wants to improve his yield and is considering growing a new crop, but he is unsure what will grow well in his soil. He relies on his own experience, advice from neighboring farmers, and agricultural shops when making decisions.

## Situation and current tools

- **Location:** A rural village in western Georgia.
- **Role:** Small-scale farmer who grows crops for income.
- **Current approach:** Uses personal experience, asks other farmers for advice, and consults agricultural shops.
- **Information gap:** Does not have enough confidence in how his soil affects crop choice and growing decisions.
- **Problem moment:** Before planting a new crop or spending money on seeds, fertilizer, and other supplies.

## Cost of the problem

Choosing an unsuitable crop or using inappropriate inputs could waste money, labor, and an entire growing season. The actual financial impact must be established through interviews.

## Who this ICP is NOT

- Large commercial farms that already employ agronomists or have dedicated soil-testing services.
- People who grow crops only for personal consumption and are not concerned about farm income.
- People who already have reliable soil analysis and professional guidance for their planting decisions.

## How we will reach five people

1. A farmer known to a team member.
2. A neighboring farmer introduced by the first contact.
3. A farmer who has tried growing a new crop.
4. A farmer who grows vegetables or berries for sale.
5. A farmer introduced through a local agricultural shop or farming contact.

These are target interviewee types, not confirmed contacts. The team must identify actual people and confirm that they are available to interview.

## Evidence and assumptions

**Current hypothesis:** Small-scale farmers need more reliable, locally relevant information about their soil before making crop-growing decisions.

**To verify:** Whether soil uncertainty is a real, frequent problem; what farmers currently do about it; how much failed decisions cost; and whether they already pay for soil testing or agronomic advice.

**Status:** Initial ICP hypothesis. Revise it after real farmer interviews.
