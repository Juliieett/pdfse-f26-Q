# Ideal Customer Profile

One person from the interviews, not a type of farmer. The portrait is Nino in log 01. Logs 07 and 10 are other people with a related problem. Their details are not mixed into hers.

**Name (code name) and one-line sketch:** Nino, a village neighbor who planted strawberries last autumn on the land behind her house. Nobody on her street has grown them. (log 01)

**Their situation:** Tomatoes and potatoes no longer pay, which is why she tried a crop nobody nearby has grown. She asked two neighbors, a cousin in Telavi who grows grapes, and the agro shop seller. She watched YouTube videos, mostly from Poland or Turkey, and later joined a Facebook gardeners group. She did not save the video she used. The interview was by phone. (log 01)

**The moment the problem shows up:** Twice in the one season. Before planting, when she needed to know when to plant. Again in spring, when the plants showed gray spots and the berries went soft with white fuzz. (log 01)

**What it costs them:** About 800 lari, for 200 seedlings, plastic, and drip tubes. About a third of the plants died and about half the berries rotted. She sold almost nothing. The family ate them. (log 01)

**What they do about it today:** She asks whoever will answer, including the person who sold her the seedlings. That seller told her to water a lot. She suspects that advice was wrong. In the Facebook group she cannot tell who knows. (log 01)

**Three details, each from a log:**
1. Nobody on her street had grown strawberries, and everyone she asked said something different. (log 01)
2. The first season cost about 800 lari, a third of the plants died, and about half the berries rotted. (log 01)
3. She still cannot tell whose advice to trust: "ვერ ვხვდები, ვინ იცის." / "I can't tell who knows." (log 01)

**What they are NOT:**
- A neighbor who only grows the crops the street already knows. She already knows the headache of selling tomatoes and potatoes. She said that only after she was asked. (log 01)
- A grower whose loss started at the seedlings, with one seller and no way to tell what she had bought. That is Manana, not Nino. (log 10)
