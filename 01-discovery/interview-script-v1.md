# Interview Script v1

**Problems we are investigating:** three problems from the pool; interviews decide which we keep.
**Language:** Georgian (English kept here in the repo)

## Opening (2 min, word for word, all problems)
"Hi, I'm [name], a student at KIU. We're learning how people deal with [topic]. I'm not selling anything and I have nothing to show you. Is it okay if I take notes? I won't use your name anywhere."

## Follow-up bank (all problems)
- And then what happened?
- Why was that a problem?
- When was the last time that happened? *(after "usually" or "always")*
- Can you show me?
- What would that let you do that you can't now? *(after a feature request)*
- *Silence. Count to three.*

## Close (2 min, all problems)
- Who else should we talk to about this?
- Could we come back when we have something to show you?

## P1 · Growing a new crop in the village (strawberries)
**Our prediction (never say it aloud):** We are wrong if villagers who tried a new crop say they easily found the advice they needed (a neighbor, an agro shop, or online) before planting.
**Who:** our neighbor, a villager who tried a new crop before, a villager who grows vegetables or berries to sell.

**Warm-up**
1. Tell me about what you grow. How long have you been doing it?

**Core**
2. Tell me about the last time you planted something you had never grown before. How did you decide on it?
3. Before planting it, where did you go to learn how to grow it? Walk me through it.
4. What went wrong that first season?
5. What did that cost you? *(push for a number: money, plants, time)*
6. Who did you ask when something went wrong? What did they tell you?
7. Tell me about the last time you sold what you grew. Where, and to whom? *(belief not picked: selling)*

**For the neighbor who has not planted yet**
- What have you done so far to prepare? Who have you talked to?
- Where did the idea of strawberries come from?
- Tell me about the last time you tried something new in your garden.

---

## P2 · Finding a reliable repairman
**Our prediction (never say it aloud):** We are wrong if people say their last repairman came when promised and did the job right the first time.
**Who:** tenants, homeowners, or parents who had a plumbing or electrical repair in the last year.

**Warm-up**
1. Tell me about your home. How long have you lived there, and who usually handles repairs?

**Core**
2. Walk me through the last time something broke and you needed a plumber or electrician.
3. How did you find the person? Who recommended them?
4. Tell me about the last time a repairman did not come when he said he would. What did you do?
5. Tell me about the last time a repair was done badly. What happened after?
6. What did that cost you? *(push for a number: money, paying twice, days without water or light)*
7. How did you agree on the price? Was the final price the same as the first one? *(belief not picked: price)*
8. Do you have someone's number saved for next time? Can you show me how you found them?

---

## P3 · Finding a flat to rent as an out-of-town student
**Our prediction (never say it aloud):** We are wrong if out-of-town students say they found their current flat within a few days, with no lost money or bad surprises.
**Who:** out-of-town classmates and first-year students.

**Warm-up**
1. Where are you from originally? How did you end up studying here?

**Core**
2. Walk me through how you found the flat you live in now, from the very first search.
3. Where did you look? Which groups, sites, or people? Can you show me?
4. How long did it take from starting to moving in? How many places did you call or visit?
5. Tell me about the last time something went wrong while searching.
6. Did you ever pay a deposit or money upfront? What happened to it?
7. What did the whole search cost you? *(push for a number: money, days, trips to the city)*
8. Tell me about the last time you had a problem with flatmates or the landlord after moving in. *(belief not picked: living there)*

## Questions we cut (red: future, hypothetical, or leading)
- "Would you use an app that teaches farming?" (hypothetical, names a product)
- "Would you pay for a service with verified repairmen?" (hypothetical future)
- "Don't you hate it when repairmen don't show up?" (leading: plants the prediction)
- "Would a student housing website help you?" (hypothetical, names a product)
- "Was searching stressful?" (yes/no, leading)
