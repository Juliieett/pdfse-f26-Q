## The script you will read from

1. Tell me about what you grow. How long have you been doing it?
2. Tell me about the last time you planted something you had never grown before. How did you decide on it?
3. The last time you bought the plants, what did the seller tell you, and what happened to those plants?
4. What went wrong after that? What did it cost you?
5. The last time two people disagreed about the same plants, what had you already paid, and whose advice did you follow?
6. Tell me about the last time you sold what you grew. Where, and to whom?
7. Who else should I talk to about this?
