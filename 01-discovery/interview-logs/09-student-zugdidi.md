# Interview 09 · first-year student from Zugdidi · (code name: Zugdidi-1)

**Week:** 4 · **Asker:** SI · **Logger:** DJ · **Consent:** yes; notes
**Real or practice:** real
**Problem:** 3 student flat

**Context:** 19, first-year at KIU, from Zugdidi. Searched in August, now lives in a shared flat.
**Our prediction:** We are wrong if out-of-town students say they found their current flat within a few days, with no lost money or bad surprises.

**Workaround:** Searches Facebook student flat groups, asks family friends, stays at her cousin's on visits, brings her dad. Showed me the groups on her phone.
**Cost:** 100 lari deposit lost, about 80 lari on buses and taxis, 3 weeks, 15 calls, 5 visits, 2 trips from Zugdidi, 1 day of her dad's work.
**Frequency:** One lost deposit and one fake-photo flat in one search (August).
**Prediction check:** Only after we asked. She mentioned duplicate posts on her own while showing me the groups, but the time and money losses came when I asked how long it took and about deposits.
**Surprise ★:** Taken flats stay posted, and the bright-photo flat looks like a way to get visitors in. Her mom was also a little angry at her, so family is part of the problem.
**Commitment:** Introduction and follow-up (yes).
