# Interview 03 · First-year student from Zugdidi, flat search · (code name: Ana-1)

**Date:** 17 Oct, 15:00 · **Asker:** DJ · **Logger:** SI · **Consent:** yes,notes
**Real or practice:** real

**Context:** First-year student from another town, accepted in July. Searched from home from early August, mainly through two Facebook groups for renting in Tbilisi. Found a flat in the end. Contacted through Facebook.
**Our prediction:** We are wrong if out-of-town students say they found their current flat within a few days, with no lost money or bad surprises.

**Workaround:** Facebook groups every evening, one contact through a cousin's friend in the city, video calls with landlords who agreed. In the end her father took a day off and they drove up and viewed four flats in one day.
**Cost:** About four weeks of evenings, plus the trip (petrol and one hotel night), about 150 lari. No deposit lost, but she nearly sent 100 lari to hold a flat she had not seen.
**Frequency:** Five or six calls about already-rented flats in one search. Currently searching again with a friend who wants a cheaper place.
**Prediction check:** No. The search took about four weeks, cost money, and nearly cost a deposit.
**Surprise ★:** The fear itself, not the money. Her family stopped the deposit transfer, so the protection was her mother saying no, not anything in the listing. Post-move-in problems (the landlady visiting unannounced, a heater broken for two weeks) are annoying, but she ranked them below the search.
**Commitment:** Offered screenshots of the Facebook groups and said everyone from her faculty who isn't from Tbilisi has the same problem. Agreed we can come back. No names yet.
**Next:** Get the screenshots. Ask her friend about the current search, since that is a second, more recent search to compare. Ask the next student how many listings were already taken. Her count of five or six is a number to test.
