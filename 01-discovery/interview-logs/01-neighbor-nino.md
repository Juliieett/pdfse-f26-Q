# Interview 01 · Neighbor, village, new crop (strawberries) · (code name: Nino-1)

**Date:** 15 Oct, 12:00 · **Asker:** DJ · **Logger:** SI · **Consent:** : yes,notes
**Real or practice:** Real

**Context:** Village neighbor who planted strawberries last autumn on land behind her house. Nobody on her street has grown them before. Tomatoes and potatoes no longer pay, which is why she tried something new. Phone interview.
**Our prediction:** We are wrong if villagers who tried a new crop say they easily found the advice they needed (a neighbor, an agro shop, or online) before planting.

**Workaround:** Called a cousin in Telavi who grows grapes, asked two neighbors and the agro shop seller, watched YouTube videos, later joined a Facebook gardeners group. Did not save the video she used.
**Cost:** About 800 lari (200 seedlings, plastic, drip tubes). About a third of the plants died and about half the berries rotted. Sold almost nothing, the family ate them and was not even that good as she said.
**Frequency:** One season so far. Problem hit twice: before planting (when to plant) and in spring (gray spots, soft berries with white fuzz).
**Prediction check:** No. She did not find trustworthy advice easily. Everyone she asked gave a different answer, and nobody had grown strawberries.
**Surprise ★:** The agro shop seller sold her the seedlings and also gave the advice ("water a lot"), and she suspects that advice was wrong. The advice came from the person selling the product. Her problem is not too little advice but too many conflicting sources and no way to tell who knows.
**Commitment:** Agreed we can come back when we have something to show. Named Lali's husband, who tried kiwi two years ago and gave up. No intro yet.
**Next:** Ask Lali's husband what he did when the kiwi failed. Ask Nino to send the group name and any screenshots. P1-7 is a weaker alternative here, not killed: she has not even reached the selling stage with strawberries.
