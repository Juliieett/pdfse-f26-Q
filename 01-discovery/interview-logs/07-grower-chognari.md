# Interview 07 · mandarin grower, wants grapefruit, Chognari · (code name: Chognari-1)

**Week:** 4 · **Asker:** SI · **Logger:** DJ · **Consent:** yes; notes
**Real or practice:** real
**Problem:** 1 new crop

**Context:** Mid-50s, about 50 mandarin trees inherited from his father, plus some corn and hazelnut. Has not planted grapefruit yet.
**Our prediction:** We are wrong if villagers who tried a new crop say they easily found the advice they needed, from a neighbor, an agro shop or online, before planting.

**Quotes** (verbatim, in the language spoken; translation beside it; ★ = surprise)
- "ჭოგნარში არავის არ აქვს, როგორც მე ვიცი." / "Nobody in Chognari has it, I'd know."
- "ნერგს შეგიკვეთავო, ოღონდ არ ვიცი აქ რომელი გაიხარებსო." / "He said he can order seedlings, but he doesn't know which kind will hold here."
- "ზურაბმა თქვა, ყინვააო. მერე სხვამ თქვა, არა, სოკოაო ან ობიაო. ახლაც არ ვიცი რომელია." / "Zurab said it's the frost. Then another guy said no, it's fungus. I still don't know which one."
- ★ "შარშან მანდარინი იაფი იყო, აბა რა გითხრა." / "Last year the mandarin was cheap, what can I say."

**Workaround:** Watches Turkish YouTube he half understands, asks the agro shop near the bridge, asks neighbor Zurab. Showed me a photo of the dead leaves: Zurab said frost, the shop said fungus, same photo.
**Cost:** 20 young trees lost (about 8 lari each, but 4 years old, so 4 years of work). Has not planted grapefruit yet.
**Frequency:** The loss happened once, two winters ago. Sells once a year, in November.
**Prediction check:** Only after we asked. He said he had found no one who grew grapefruit only when I asked if he had asked someone.
**Surprise ★:** The idea of grapefruit comes from a bad mandarin price, so the real problem may be selling, not growing. He can't tell frost from disease even on his current crop.
**Commitment:** Follow-up (invited me back for coffee) and an introduction.
**Next:** Talk to Zurab (neighbor) and the woman in Shukhuti whose husband tried kiwi. Ask both: "Tell me about the last time you planted something new. Where did you get advice?" (Georgian: "ბოლოს როდის დარგე რამე ახალი? ვისთან იკითხე რჩევა?")
