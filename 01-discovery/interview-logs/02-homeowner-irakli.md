
# Interview 02 · Homeowner, plumber no-show · (code name: Irakli-1)

**Date:** 16 Oct, 14:00 · **Asker:** DJ · **Logger:** SI · **Consent:** yes,notes 
**Real or practice:** Real

**Context:** Homeowner. In August a pipe under the kitchen sink leaked. He found a plumber through his wife's colleague. Last month a hall socket sparked and he used a different route. Phone interview.
**Our prediction:** We are wrong if people say their last repairman came when promised and did the job right the first time.

**Workaround:** Got the plumber's number from his wife's colleague. Next time (electrician) asked the building's Viber group and two people recommended the same man. Can show the messages, but they are only "call this number."
**Cost:** Three days without water in the kitchen, half a day off work waiting. Paid 80 lari, then 120 to a second plumber to fix it properly. 200 lari total.
**Frequency:** Two repair events in the last two months: plumber (bad), electrician (good). Others' experiences are secondhand.
**Prediction check:** No for the last plumber (late, and the job failed within a week). But the most recent repairman, the electrician, came on time and did it right.
**Surprise ★:** He did not argue about the price because he needed water, so the customer has no leverage once the pipe is already leaking. Also, the Viber group recommendation worked and was repeated by two people, which differs from a single colleague's referral.
**Commitment:** Mentioned brother-in-law and downstairs neighbor as people with the same problem. No intro yet. Agreed we can come back.
**Next:** Ask the brother-in-law and the neighbor about their last no-show, with the exact day. Test in later interviews whether the real problem is reliability (P2) or the surprise price (P2-7). Both appeared here, and the price only after prompting.
