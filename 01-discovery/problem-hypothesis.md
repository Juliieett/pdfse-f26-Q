## The three problems we chose

We chose these three problems from `00-foundation/problem-pool.md` (Sandro's entries) and the team works on all three. We interview people about each one; the interviews decide which one we keep, and the pick goes in `DECISIONS.md`.

1. **Growing a new crop in the village:** a villager wants to grow something new (our neighbor, strawberries), but nobody nearby has grown it before, so there is nobody to learn from or ask when something goes wrong.
2. **Finding a reliable repairman:** a plumber or electrician says "I'll come tomorrow" and does not show up, or does the job badly, so it has to be done and paid for twice.
3. **Finding a flat to rent as an out-of-town student:** students from other towns spend weeks calling numbers and searching Facebook groups for a flat, and some lose a deposit.

## Problem 1 · Growing a new crop in the village (strawberries)

### Step 2: The hypothesis we test first
> **We believe** villagers who want to start growing a crop nobody nearby has grown before (our neighbor, planning strawberries)
> **struggle with** getting advice they can trust about how to grow it
> **when** they prepare to plant it for the first time, and again when something goes wrong in the first season
> **which costs them** money on seedlings and supplies, and part or all of the first harvest
> **Today they** ask neighbors who have never grown it, ask the agro shop seller, or watch videos
> **We are wrong if** villagers who tried a new crop say they easily found the advice they needed (a neighbor, an agro shop, or online) before planting.

### Step 3: The beliefs we did not pick
- Selling the harvest is the real problem, not growing it → script question P1-7

## Problem 2 · Finding a reliable repairman

### Step 2: The hypothesis we test first
> **We believe** tenants, homeowners, and parents who needed a plumber or electrician in the last year
> **struggle with** finding a repairman who comes when he says and does the job right the first time
> **when** something breaks at home and they call the person someone recommended
> **which costs them** days without water or electricity, and paying twice when the job has to be redone
> **Today they** ask family, neighbors, or a Facebook group for a number and call whoever answers
> **We are wrong if** people say their last repairman came when promised and did the job right the first time.

### Step 3: The beliefs we did not pick
- The final price being higher than agreed is the real problem, not reliability → script question P2-7

---

## Problem 3 · Finding a flat to rent as an out-of-town student

### Step 2: The hypothesis we test first
> **We believe** students from other towns, especially first-years
> **struggle with** finding a flat they can trust without being in the city to see it
> **when** they search before the semester starts, through Facebook groups and phone calls
> **which costs them** weeks of searching, trips to the city, and sometimes a lost deposit
> **Today they** post and scroll in Facebook groups, call listings one by one, and ask friends already in the city
> **We are wrong if** out-of-town students say they found their current flat within a few days, with no lost money or bad surprises.

### Step 3: The beliefs we did not pick
- Problems with flatmates or the landlord after moving in are the real problem, not finding the flat → script question P3-8
