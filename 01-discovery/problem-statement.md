# Nergi — Problem Statement v1

Small-scale farmers may struggle to determine whether a new crop will grow successfully under their local conditions (Log 04, Log 07).

Farmers may not receive enough reliable information about planting materials and growing requirements before investing money (Log 10).

When farmers seek advice, the information they receive may be incomplete, difficult to understand, or contradictory (Log 04, Log 07, Log 10).

This uncertainty can contribute to wasted money, lost growing time, and difficulty making informed crop-related decisions (Log 07, Log 10).

Farmers need a dependable way to access and verify relevant crop information before deciding what to plant, buy, or do next (Log 04, Log 07, Log 10).
