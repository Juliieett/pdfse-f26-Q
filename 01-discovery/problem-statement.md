# Problem Statement

## The statement

1. **Who:** Nino, a village neighbor who planted strawberries last autumn on the land behind her house, where nobody on the street had grown them, and Manana, who bought kiwi plants from the only seller who had them. (01, 10)
2. **When:** At the moment they pay the seller, and again when the plants fail: gray spots and soft berries in Nino's spring, and two years of kiwi plants with no fruit. (01, 10)
3. **Cost:** About 800 lari for Nino, a third of the plants dead and about half the berries rotted. 360 lari and then 200 lari for Manana, and two years with no fruit. The Chognari grower lost 20 young trees, about 8 lari each, and four years of growth. (01, 07, 10)
4. **Today:** Nino asked two neighbors, a cousin in Telavi who grows grapes, the agro shop that sold her the seedlings, YouTube, and a Facebook gardeners group. Manana bought a second batch from a woman who explained the male and female plants. The Chognari grower asked Zurab and the shop near the bridge. (01, 07, 10)
5. **Why it fails:** "Everyone says something different." "I can't tell who knows." (01) "I had no way to know what I was buying until nothing grew fruit." "Nobody told us the ratio." (10) "Zurab said it's the frost. Then another guy said no, it's fungus. I still don't know which one." (07)

## What the evidence ruled out

The prediction was that villagers who tried a new crop would say they could not find advice they trusted before planting. One of three logs raised that without being asked (01). Logs 07 and 10 raised it only after they were asked. On their own, log 07 raised a bad mandarin price, and log 10 raised the seedlings. Weakened, not confirmed. See the Week 3 new-crop line in `DECISIONS.md`.

A missing soil test was the earlier Week 4 line. None of logs 01, 07, or 10 name a soil test.

## What is still open

No log shows Nino with two answers in front of her, saying which one she followed last spring. She also said not to bring her a complicated phone thing (01). That is the behavior the prototype has to test, on paper.

## Pattern behind it

- Pattern: The person who sells the plants is also the person who explains them, and the buyer cannot tell what they bought until the plants fail. (01, 07, 10)
- Root cause: They learn the plants were the wrong ones only after the money is spent, because the seller was the explanation. (01, 10)
