# Prototype Plan

From `01-discovery/problem-statement.md`. Paper only. Log 01 said not to bring a complicated phone thing.

## 1. The riskiest assumption

> We assume that Nino, shown two pieces of advice from her own season written on paper, will point to the one she followed and say what happened to the plants after.

**Why this one is riskiest (cite a log, or say "no log yet, that is why we test it"):** No log shows her choosing between two answers that are both in front of her (01). If she will not do that on paper, there is nothing to learn from building a comparison.

## 2. The one action

> A grower who already lost a first season can point to the advice they followed.

**Will not appear, even if a tool generates it** (list what you expect to be tempted by):
- A phone screen, a chat, or a page that tells them which card is correct.
- A soil-test result. None of logs 01, 07, or 10 name one.
- Advice for a crop they have not already tried to grow.

## 5. The test that can fail

Write this before the first session, and do not edit it after.

- **Task, read aloud to the tester, word for word:** "Here are two things people told you about your plants. Point to the one you followed. Then tell me what the plants did after that, and what you had already paid."
- **Success looks like** (an observable behavior, with a time limit): Within two minutes, they put a finger on one card and name a loss that matches their own season, without asking us which card is right.
- **Failure looks like** (an observable behavior, at least two): They ask us which card is correct. They will not choose a card. They talk about a phone or about a crop they have not planted.
- **Decision rule** (out of 5 testers, how many failures change your plan, and to what): If 3 or more of 5 fail, the comparison is the wrong test. The next test is the seedlings question from log 10: what the seller said at the moment they paid.
- **Our prediction of the first thing a tester gets wrong:** They ask us which advice is the right one, instead of saying which one they followed.
